Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Edupoly respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use our learning platform, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the platform, we may collect:

  • Information you provide, such as your name, email address, account details, and support messages.
  • Learning activity, including courses viewed, progress, quiz attempts, and results.
  • Technical data such as browser type, device information, IP address, and diagnostic logs.

How we use information

We use information to provide and improve the learning experience, maintain accounts, track course progress, respond to requests, secure the platform, diagnose problems, and comply with legal obligations.

Cookies and local storage

We may use cookies or similar browser storage to keep you signed in, remember preferences, understand platform usage, and support essential functionality. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may affect some features.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us host, secure, analyze, or operate the platform. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business reorganization.

Data retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy. We use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, or object to certain uses. You may also update browser permissions and cookie settings at any time.

Children's privacy

The platform is not intended for children who cannot legally consent to data processing in their region without a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, please contact Edupoly so the matter can be reviewed.

Third-party links

Learning materials may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and Edupoly is not responsible for those external services.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, please contact Edupoly through the contact details provided on the official Edupoly website or learning platform.
